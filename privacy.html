Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Wordfront respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Wordfront Discord Activity and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

Wordfront may receive limited information from Discord and gameplay activity, including:

  • Scores, match results, and gameplay statistics

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and improve Wordfront, including creating game sessions, displaying player names, tracking scores, maintaining gameplay functionality, and improving the game experience.

Data Sharing

Wordfront does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be shared only when required by law or necessary to operate the service.

Game-related information may be stored on servers used to operate Wordfront. Reasonable efforts are made to protect stored information.

Children’s Privacy

Wordfront is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. Users must comply with Discord’s age requirements.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Wordfront after updates means you accept the revised policy.
