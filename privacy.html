Wordfront Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Wordfront respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Wordfront Discord Activity and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

Wordfront may receive limited information provided by Discord, including:

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Username and Display Name
  • Game-related information such as scores, match results, and gameplay statistics

How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Provide gameplay functionality
  • Create and manage game sessions
  • Display player information during matches
  • Maintain leaderboards and game statistics
  • Improve the Wordfront experience

Data Sharing

Wordfront does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties except as required by law.

Data Storage

Game-related information may be stored on servers used to operate Wordfront. Reasonable efforts are made to protect stored information.

Children's Privacy

Wordfront is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Wordfront after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
